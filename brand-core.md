# RAW Actor Studio Brand Core

## Current Brand Read

RAW is not positioned as a soft, inspirational acting school. The public-facing promise is sharper:

1. Clarity over vagueness
2. Authorship over guessing
3. Autonomy over dependency — actors leave with tools they own, not a method they need a coach to unlock
4. Repeatable tools over hype
5. Industry reality over hobbyist language
6. Serious support over ego or teardown teaching

The strongest current phrases from official RAW pages are:

- Action. Intention. Purpose.
- Train with clarity.
- Claim authorship of every performance.
- All Levels, Genuinely Welcome.
- Lineage-Based, Contemporary Teaching.
- A Clear, Repeatable System.
- Individualized Coaching, Not One-Size-Fits-All.
- Training That Meets Industry Reality.
- A Serious, Supportive Community.

## Message Hierarchy

Use this order when writing content:

1. Core promise: RAW gives actors clear, repeatable tools.
2. Practical result: actors stop guessing and start making usable choices under pressure.
3. Differentiator: the training is lineage-based, contemporary, and applied to real film/TV/audition conditions.
4. Tone of the room: serious, respectful, supportive, and individualized.
5. Conversion: class booking, free audit, or RAW Reels inquiry.

## Audience Definitions

### Emerging Actor

- May be new or early in training
- Feels uncertain in self-tapes, auditions, or scene work
- Needs strong foundations, practical tools, and language that does not talk down to them

### Working / Established Actor

- Already has training, credits, or representation
- Wants more consistency, speed, precision, and competitive sharpness
- Responds to concrete craft language, industry realism, and proof of rigor

### Career-Reset / Returning Actor

- Has prior experience but wants a better system or updated process
- Needs clear structure, not empty motivation

## Avoid / Ban

Do not publish copy that leans on:

- follow your dreams
- chase your passion
- unlock your potential
- become the best version of yourself
- level up your acting
- confidence without a concrete acting context
- authenticity without a concrete acting context
- generic empowerment
- fake urgency
- invented testimonials
- invented booking claims
- abstract craft talk with no example

## AI Slop Guardrails

Every post must include:

1. One concrete acting problem or scenario
2. One concrete tool or lens
3. One proof point, source note, or brand anchor
4. One clear CTA at most

Reject content if:

- it could belong to any Toronto acting studio
- it says a lot but teaches nothing
- it sounds polished but empty
- it repeats recent hooks or sentence shapes
- it uses a location, price, or class fact that is not marked verified
